DO vs DON'T

DO THIS

✓ Start with Sources Sought — respond to every one in your NAICS
✓ Contact SBLOs — build relationships before opportunities post
✓ Team with primes — leverage their past performance and vehicles
✓ Use data — target agencies based on spending patterns and pain points

DON'T DO THIS

✗ Spray and pray on SAM — bidding everything without research
✗ Skip market research — guessing instead of using real spending data
✗ Bid without teaming — going solo when you lack past performance
✗ Wait for the perfect opportunity — analysis paralysis kills momentum
