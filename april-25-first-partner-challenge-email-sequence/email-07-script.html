THE SCRIPT

The Phone Script That Gets Meetings

{{FirstName}},

People overthink the phone call.

Here's the reality: You're not selling anything. You're bringing them an opportunity.

THE MINDSET

"I have nothing to sell. I'm looking for someone I can trust to work with on long-term prospective deals."

The Script (When They Answer):

WHEN THEY ANSWER:

"Hi, my name is [YOUR NAME]. I have a market research request from the government — [AGENCY] is looking for companies that do [WORK TYPE]. I'm seeing if you'd be a good fit as a teaming partner."

THE HOOK:

"My company can handle a portion of this. I'm looking for someone who can do [THEIR SPECIALTY]. Would you have 15 minutes this week to discuss?"

Key Phrases That Work:

→ "I found an opportunity I wanted to share with you"
→ "I'm willing to do all the upfront work"
→ "Looking for companies ready, willing, and able"
→ "What portion could your company handle?"
→ "Can we schedule 15 minutes to discuss?"

Goal: Get a MEETING, not close a deal

Your Practice Assignment:

1. Read the script out loud 3 times
2. Fill in YOUR name and YOUR NAICS specialty
3. Practice once with a friend or family member
