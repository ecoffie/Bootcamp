What Made This Work

1 Started with data, not guessing. Market Assassin identified the right agencies from day one.
2 Contacted buyers BEFORE the RFP. Built relationships months before the solicitation dropped.
3 Used a prime as a warm introduction. The SBLO made the introduction to the contracting officer.
4 Responded to Sources Sought when nobody else did. Being in the top 5% who respond changes everything.
5 Built the relationship over months, not days. Trust takes time. They invested 7 months of consistent effort.

What DIDN'T Work

X Cold-calling contracting officers — no response. They don't pick up the phone for unknown callers.
X Bidding on full & open competitions — too much competition. Dozens of established firms with past performance.
X Generic capability statements — ignored. One-size-fits-all documents go straight to the trash.

Only targeted, data-driven outreach worked.
