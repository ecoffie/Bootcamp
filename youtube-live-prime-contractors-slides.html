What is an SBLO?

Small Business Liaison Officer

1
Every large prime with government contracts over $750K must have one
2
Their JOB is to find small businesses to subcontract with
3
They WANT to hear from you — it helps them meet their goals
4
They can connect you to program managers and proposal teams

The SBLO is your front door into every prime contractor.

What Primes Want to Hear

DO Say

+ "We specialize in NAICS 541512 with Secret clearance"
+ "We're 8(a) certified and can help you meet SB goals"
+ "We noticed your [specific contract] — we can support that scope"

DON'T Say

X "We can do anything you need"
X "We're a new company looking for our first contract"
X "Can you just add us to your vendor list?"

Primes want SPECIFIC capabilities that fill gaps.

SBLO Outreach Template

Subject: Small Business Teaming Partner — [Your NAICS] — [Your Company Name]

Hi [SBLO Name],


My name is [Your Name] with [Company]. We are a [certification(s)] small business specializing in [NAICS code and description].


We have [brief past performance or capability summary] and hold [clearance level, if applicable].


We'd love to explore teaming or subcontracting opportunities with [Prime Name]. Would you have 15 minutes for a call this week?


I've attached our capability statement for your review.


Best regards,
[Your Name] | [Phone] | [Email]

Short. Specific. Professional. Send this to 10 SBLOs this week.
