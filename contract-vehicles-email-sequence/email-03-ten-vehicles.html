10 Contract Vehicles Every Contractor Should Know

1. GSA MAS (Multiple Award Schedule)

The grandfather of all vehicles. Over $50B annually.

2. OASIS+ (Services)

GSA's next-gen professional services vehicle. Just opened.

3. 8(a) STARS III

$50B ceiling for 8(a) IT services.

4. Alliant 2 / Alliant 3

$75B ceiling for IT solutions.

5. SEWP V / SEWP VI

NASA's IT hardware/software vehicle. Extremely popular.

6. CIO-SP4

NIH's IT services vehicle. Recently awarded.

7. VETS 2 (VA)

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business set-aside.

8. HCaTS (Human Capital)

$25B ceiling for HR and training services.

9. GSA PSHC (Facilities)

Facilities and construction services.

10. Agency-Specific BPAs

Each agency has their own. DHS, DoD, HHS - all have vehicles.

Key insight: You don't need to be ON a vehicle to win work. You can partner with someone who is, or respond to Sources Sought to position for the next one.
