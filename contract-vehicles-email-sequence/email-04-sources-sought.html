What is a Sources Sought?

A Sources Sought is the government saying: "We're thinking about buying this. Who's out there that can do it?"

It's NOT a solicitation. There's no contract being awarded. But here's what most contractors don't realize:

Why Sources Sought Responses Matter:

1. Your response influences whether it becomes a set-aside

2. You get on the CO's radar BEFORE the solicitation drops

3. You learn what's coming 30-90 days before everyone else

4. You can shape the requirements through your feedback

Real example: One of our members responded to a Sources Sought. The agency saw enough small business responses that they made it an 8(a) set-aside. That member won a $4.2M task order - because they raised their hand early.
