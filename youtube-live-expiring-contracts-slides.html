3 Mistakes With Expiring Contracts

1 Waiting for the solicitation to drop — by then, the incumbent and their team are already locked in. You're 18 months late.
2 Ignoring the incumbent — they're not your enemy. They're your potential teaming partner. Contact them BEFORE the recompete.
3 Not responding to Sources Sought — less than 5% of contractors respond. If you do, the agency knows you exist. That matters.

The Right Way to Use This Data

1 Filter by YOUR NAICS — find the 10–20 contracts most relevant to what you do
2 Research the incumbent — who has the contract now? What's their size? Are they a good teaming partner?
3 Contact the agency SBLO — introduce yourself. Ask about the recompete timeline. Get on their radar.
4 Respond to Sources Sought — when the agency posts one, be in the top 5% who respond
5 Approach the incumbent for teaming — use the Federal Contractor Database to find SBLO contacts
