I'm new to federal contracting. Is this too advanced?

No. While we cover IDIQ vehicles including GSA, most of what you'll learn applies to ALL federal contracts. Expiring contracts exist in every acquisition method - not just GSA.

This March Surge teaches you how to FIND opportunities (expiring contracts, IDIQs, teaming). The April Bootcamp teaches you how to WIN them (proposal writing, pricing, past performance). They're complementary - find first, then write to win.
