Your Phone Script for Finding Teaming Partners

Before You Call

  • Research the company on SAM.gov (NAICS, set-asides, past contracts)
  • Have your capability statement ready to send
  • Know YOUR value proposition (what you bring to a team)
  • Have this script printed or on screen
  • Quiet space, phone charged, notepad ready

The Opening (15 seconds)

Say This: "Hi, this is [YOUR NAME] with [YOUR COMPANY]. I'm a small business in federal contracting, and I came across your company while researching [THEIR NAICS or INDUSTRY]. Do you have 2 minutes for a quick question?"
Why This Works: You're not selling anything. You're asking a question. Most people will say yes to 2 minutes.

The Bridge (30 seconds)

If They Say Yes: "Great, thanks. I saw that you've done work with [AGENCY or CONTRACT] and your capabilities in [AREA]. I'm looking to connect with companies who might be open to teaming on future opportunities. Are you currently exploring teaming relationships for 2026?"
If They're Busy: "Totally understand. Is there a better time I could call back? Or I can send a quick email with my capability statement?"

The Value Exchange (60 seconds)

When They Ask "What Do You Do?": "We're a [YOUR CERT] [YOUR SIZE] business specializing in [YOUR NAICS/SERVICES]. We've done [YOUR EXPERIENCE - contracts, past performance, relevant work]. I'm specifically looking for partners who [ONE OF THESE]: - Need our certification for set-aside requirements - Could use our technical capabilities as part of a team - Are on contract vehicles we could subcontract through Would any of those scenarios fit your business?"

The Close (30 seconds)

If Interested:

"That's great to hear. What's the best way to continue this conversation? Can I send you my capability statement and schedule a 15-minute call this week to explore specifics?"

If Not Right Now:

"I appreciate the honesty. Would you be open to connecting on LinkedIn or staying in touch for future opportunities? Sometimes timing just has to align."

Handling Objections

"We're not looking for partners right now"

"Completely understand. Things change quickly in GovCon. Mind if I send my capability statement for when opportunities do come up?"

"Send me an email"

"Will do. What email should I use, and is there anything specific you'd want me to address?"

"What's in it for us?"

"Fair question. I can bring [YOUR CERT] for set-aside compliance, [YOUR SKILL] for technical depth, and I'm looking for mutually beneficial relationships, not just a name on a proposal."

"We only work with companies we know"

"That makes sense. This call is the start of getting to know each other. Would a 15-minute video call help you learn more about our capabilities?"

After the Call

  • Log the call in your tracking sheet immediately
  • Send follow-up email within 24 hours (with capability statement)
  • Connect on LinkedIn with a personalized note
  • Set reminder for follow-up if they requested later contact
  • Note any specific opportunities they mentioned
