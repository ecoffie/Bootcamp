Recompete Positioning Checklist

GovCon Giants | March 2026 Surge

Use this 12-18 month timeline to position for expiring federal contracts. Check off each item as you complete it.

PHASE 1: RESEARCH (18-12 Months Out)

Contract Identification

  • Identify 10-20 expiring contracts in your NAICS code
  • Filter by contract value (your target range)
  • Filter by agency (agencies you want to work with)
  • Note expiration dates and plan backwards

Incumbent Research

  • Research current contract holder (prime contractor)
  • Identify incumbent's size status (small, large, etc.)
  • Research incumbent's past performance on this contract
  • Identify incumbent's strengths and weaknesses
  • Determine if incumbent is a potential teaming partner

Scope Analysis

  • Read the original solicitation (if available)
  • Review contract modifications and task orders
  • Understand the scope of work performed
  • Identify any pain points or gaps in performance
  • Research agency satisfaction with current contractor

PHASE 2: POSITIONING (12-6 Months Out)

Agency Outreach

  • Identify the Contracting Officer for this contract
  • Identify the Small Business Liaison Officer (SBLO)
  • Request a meeting with the SBLO
  • Introduce your company and capabilities
  • Ask about the recompete timeline

Sources Sought Response

  • Monitor SAM.gov for Sources Sought notices
  • Prepare Sources Sought response template
  • Submit response within deadline
  • Include capability statement
  • Highlight relevant past performance

Industry Day Participation

  • Monitor for Industry Day announcements
  • Register and attend Industry Days
  • Prepare questions about the requirement
  • Network with other potential bidders
  • Follow up with contacts made

Teaming Strategy

  • Identify potential teaming partners
  • Approach incumbent about subcontracting (if appropriate)
  • Approach other primes about teaming
  • Execute teaming agreements
  • Define roles and responsibilities

PHASE 3: PRE-SOLICITATION (6-0 Months Out)

Team Finalization

  • Finalize teaming arrangements
  • Execute formal teaming agreement
  • Assign proposal roles to team members
  • Identify key personnel for the contract
  • Obtain commitment letters from key personnel

Capability Statement Update

  • Update capability statement for this specific contract
  • Tailor to agency's needs and language
  • Include relevant past performance
  • Highlight differentiators
  • Review and finalize

Draft Technical Approach

  • Outline your technical approach
  • Identify your discriminators (what makes you different)
  • Document your management approach
  • Prepare staffing plan framework
  • Develop transition plan framework

Past Performance Preparation

  • Identify 3-5 relevant past performance references
  • Contact references and confirm participation
  • Prepare past performance narratives (STAR format)
  • Document metrics and outcomes
  • Obtain performance ratings if available

PHASE 4: PROPOSAL (After RFP Release)

Compliance Matrix

  • Create compliance matrix from RFP requirements
  • Map every requirement to your response
  • Assign section owners
  • Track completion status
  • Verify 100% compliance before submission

Proposal Development

  • Develop detailed technical approach
  • Write management approach section
  • Complete past performance volume
  • Develop pricing (competitive and profitable)
  • Include all required certifications

Review & Submission

  • Conduct internal compliance review
  • Conduct internal quality review (Red Team)
  • Address all review comments
  • Final compliance check
  • Submit before deadline (never on deadline day!)
