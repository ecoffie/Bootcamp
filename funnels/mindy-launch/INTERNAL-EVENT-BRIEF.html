What this is (in one line)

Mindy is the AI BD analyst for federal small businesses — she reads the solicitation, knows the incumbent's real contract, finds who's buying, and drafts the response. Grounded in real government data (USASpending, SAM, SBA, GAO), not generic AI.

The Mindy Launch is the full, guided live demo of everything Mindy does — a hands-on working session, not a webinar. Mindy is already live at getmindy.ai; July 25 is where we walk the whole product end to end. It's the payoff of a 5-part YouTube runway series.
