Anatomy of a Winning Task Order Proposal

Section 1

Technical Approach

Your PLAN to execute THIS specific task. Methodology, deliverables, milestones. Specific to the SOW. This is where you win or lose.

Section 2

Staffing & Management

WHO does the work. Qualifications, org chart, PM identified. Real people — not "TBD." Agencies want to know you're ready NOW.

Section 3

Past Performance

Similar work. Same size, scope, complexity. Federal preferred — but commercial counts if framed correctly. Quality over quantity.

Section 4

Price / Cost Volume

Match the contract type. T&M = loaded rates + hours. FFP = firm number + breakdown. Realistic. Competitive. Profitable.
