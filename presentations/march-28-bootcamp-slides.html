What is a Contract Vehicle?

Vehicle Types: The Big 4

Type What It Is Example
IDIQ Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity OASIS+, Alliant 2
GWAC Government-Wide Acquisition Contract CIO-SP3, SEWP V
BPA Blanket Purchase Agreement Agency-specific BPAs
FSS/MAS Federal Supply Schedule GSA Schedule
8

IDIQ vs. BPA vs. GWAC

IDIQ

  • Contract with ceiling value
  • Minimum guaranteed ($)
  • Task orders compete among holders

GWAC

  • IDIQ usable government-wide
  • Managed by specific agency
  • Broader use authority
BPA: Simpler agreement, no guaranteed minimum, often agency-specific
9

The GSA Schedule (MAS)

  • Multiple Award Schedule — the "big catalog"
  • 5-year base + three 5-year options (20 years total)
  • Any federal agency can buy directly
  • Simplified ordering procedures under $250K
  • Key: You set your rates, but they're public
Pro Tip: GSA Schedule is often the first vehicle small businesses pursue
10

How Task Orders Work

1️⃣
Agency identifies needWork fits vehicle scope
2️⃣
Fair OpportunityAll vehicle holders can compete
3️⃣
Short timeline2-4 weeks to respond
4️⃣
AwardBest value wins the task order
11

Fair Opportunity Exceptions

When agencies CAN direct award without competition:

  • Only one contractor can do it (unique capability)
  • Urgent need — can't wait for competition
  • Follow-on work to prior task order
  • Order is under $3,500 (micro-purchase)
  • Small business set-aside on the vehicle

Why Respond to Sources Sought?

  • Get on the agency's radar before RFP drops
  • Influence whether it's set-aside or full & open
  • Understand what the agency actually needs
  • Build relationship with the CO
  • Position for teaming if needed

What Your Response Should Include

Always Include

  • Company overview (1 para)
  • Relevant capabilities
  • Similar past performance
  • NAICS, size, certifications

If Appropriate

  • Questions for the CO
  • Suggested approach
  • Teaming interest
  • Concerns about requirements

Session 6

ON vs. THROUGH Vehicles

Your Strategic Decision

4:00 PM - 4:45 PM
53

The Big Question

Get ON a Vehicle

Apply to become a prime contractor on OASIS+, GSA MAS, etc.

Work THROUGH a Vehicle

Subcontract to someone who's already on it

54

When to Get ON a Vehicle

  • You have significant past performance to qualify
  • You'll pursue enough task orders to justify the effort
  • Your NAICS codes are a strong match
  • You have bandwidth for the application process
  • You want to prime, not sub
55

When to Work THROUGH

  • You're still building past performance
  • The vehicle application is closed or too competitive
  • You want to test the market before investing
  • Subcontracting margins work for your business
  • You have a strong prime relationship
56

💰 Subcontracting: The Money Reality

What primes actually pay subs (and what you keep):

Your Hourly Rate Prime's Cut You Receive Notes
$150/hr (your cost) 10-15% $127-135/hr Standard sub markup
$150/hr (your cost) 20-25% $112-120/hr If prime manages you heavily
$150/hr (your cost) 30%+ <$105/hr 🚩 Red flag — walk away
Rule of Thumb: If prime takes >20%, they better be providing significant BD, facilities, or clearances
57

🤝 How to Approach Primes

📝 Subcontract vs. Teaming Agreement

Factor Subcontract Teaming Agreement
When Used After prime wins Before proposal submitted
Binding? Yes — you're doing the work Usually non-binding intent

🚩 Red Flags in Sub Agreements

Walk Away If:

  • Prime takes 30%+ of your rate
  • "Exclusivity" blocks other work
  • No defined scope of work
  • Payment terms >60 days
  • They want your rates but no commitment

Good Signs:

  • Clear work share percentage
  • Net 30 payment terms
  • Named in proposal as key sub
  • Flow-down of prime's protections
  • Right to negotiate task orders
