What is an IDIQ?

IDIQ = Indefinite Delivery
Indefinite Quantity

A contract vehicle that lets agencies order services/products without re-competing every time.

  • Pre-negotiated terms & rates
  • Pre-vetted pool of contractors
  • Task orders awarded in weeks, not months
  • Multi-year with huge ceiling values

Think of it like...

A "preferred vendor" list. Once you're on, agencies can buy from you directly without the full procurement process.

Why Agencies Love Them

Speed. A full-and-open competition takes 6-12 months. A task order can be awarded in 2-4 weeks.

Types of Contract Vehicles

GWAC (Government-Wide Acquisition Contract)

Any agency can use. Example: OASIS+, 8(a) STARS III, Alliant

Agency-Specific IDIQ

Only that agency uses. Example: VA T4NG, DHS EAGLE II

BPA (Blanket Purchase Agreement)

Simplified ordering for recurring needs. Often under $7M.

GSA Schedule (MAS)

The grandfather of all vehicles. Pre-approved pricing catalog.

#1: GSA MAS (Multiple Award Schedule)

  • Ceiling: Unlimited
  • Spending: $50B+ annually
  • Categories: IT, Professional Services, Facilities, more
  • Duration: 20-year contracts
  • Who can compete: Anyone (open application)

Best For

Companies wanting a "catch-all" vehicle. If agencies can buy it, it's probably on GSA.

How to Get On

Apply through GSA eOffer. Takes 6-12 months. Need 2+ years operating history.

#2: OASIS+ (Professional Services)

  • Ceiling: No Cap (unlimited)
  • Categories: Management consulting, engineering, logistics, R&D
  • Duration: 10 years
  • Pools: Unrestricted, SB, 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, WOSB

Why It's Hot

Replaced OASIS. Just reopened for applications in 2025. This is the professional services GWAC.

Requirements

Need relevant experience, past performance, and must meet minimum revenue/contract thresholds.

#3: 8(a) STARS III

  • Ceiling: $50B
  • Focus: IT services & solutions
  • Duration: 10 years (through 2032)
  • Who can compete: 8(a) certified ONLY

The 8(a) Advantage

If you're 8(a) certified and do IT work, this is your fast track. Sole-source up to $5.5M!

Application Status

Currently accepting applications. Get in while you can - 8(a) certification expires after 9 years.

Two Paths to Vehicle Work

Get ON the Vehicle

  • Apply when vehicle opens
  • Meet all requirements
  • Wait for approval (months)
  • Compete for task orders
  • Prime contractor status

Best for: Established companies with past performance

Work THROUGH the Vehicle

  • Partner with holder as sub
  • No application needed
  • Start immediately
  • Build past performance
  • Subcontractor role

Best for: New entrants building track record

Where to Find Task Orders

SAM.gov

Filter by "Contract Type" → "Delivery/Task Order". Look for vehicle names in title.

GSA eBuy

For GSA Schedule task orders. Must have GSA contract to see all opportunities.

Vehicle-Specific Portals

OASIS+, SEWP, and others have their own ordering portals for task orders.

Direct from Primes

If you're subbing, the prime will alert you to task orders they're pursuing.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying to every vehicle (spread too thin)
  • Ignoring NAICS alignment (can't bid if not covered)
  • Waiting to be "ready" (you can sub while building)
  • Not tracking recompetes (vehicles expire and re-open)
  • Skipping Sources Sought (influences set-asides)
