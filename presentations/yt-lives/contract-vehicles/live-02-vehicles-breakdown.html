Pro Tip: Filter by NAICS in Excel

Step-by-Step

  1. Open the Active IDIQ Vehicles List
  2. Select the NAICS column
  3. Data → Filter
  4. Click filter dropdown
  5. Search for your NAICS (e.g., "541512")
  6. Review matching vehicles

Partial Match Works!

Search "5415" to see all IT-related vehicles. Search "236" for all construction.

Multiple NAICS?

Most vehicles cover multiple codes. Filter by your PRIMARY NAICS first, then review which vehicles cover your secondaries.

SLIDE 11

Set-Aside Pools Explained

8(a)

Minority-owned, economically disadvantaged. Sole-source up to $5.5M (Oct 2025 update).

SDVOSB

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned. VA prefers these.

HUBZone

Located in Historically Underutilized Business Zone.

WOSB/EDWOSB

Women-Owned Small Business. Economically disadvantaged adds benefits.

Small Business (SB)

General small business pool. Meet size standards.

Unrestricted

Open to all. Large businesses compete here.
