SLIDE 4

What is a Sources Sought?

It's Market Research

The contracting officer wants to know:

  • Are there capable companies out there?
  • Are there enough small businesses?
  • What's the competitive landscape?
  • Should this be a set-aside?

NOT a Solicitation

No contract is being awarded. You're just saying "I'm interested and capable."

Why Respond?

Your response helps determine if it becomes a set-aside. Get on the CO's radar early!

SLIDE 5

The Power of Your Response

If Enough Small Businesses Respond...

  • CO may set-aside for small business
  • May create 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB pool
  • Competition narrows significantly
  • Large businesses excluded

If You DON'T Respond...

  • CO may go full & open
  • Large businesses compete
  • You miss early positioning
  • No chance to influence requirements

Real story: One of our members responded, it became an 8(a) set-aside, they won $4.2M.

SLIDE 7

Flipping a Requirement: 8(a) → Your Set-Aside

The FAR 8(a) follow-on lock is GONE — here's how to capitalize

Step 1: Sources Sought Response

  • Monitor SAM.gov for Sources Sought notices
  • Respond even if you won't bid — shapes perception
  • Clearly state your socioeconomic status (SDVOSB, HUBZone, WOSB)
  • Demonstrate capability + past performance

Step 2: Make the Case

  • Reference Rule of Two — 2+ can compete = MUST set aside
  • Show the CO there's competition in YOUR category
  • Request 1-on-1 meeting with buying office
  • Build SBLO relationship — they advocate for you

The Opportunity

Contracts that were locked to 8(a) for years are now open for recompete under YOUR set-aside.

Pro Tips

  • Get CO name from SAM.gov listing
  • Include ALL certifications
  • Double-check your CAGE & UEI
  • Match the solicitation number exactly
SLIDE 8

Section 2: Statement of Interest

STATEMENT OF INTEREST:

[Company Name] is pleased to express our interest in the above-referenced Sources Sought. We are a [certification status] company with extensive experience in [relevant service area].

We believe we are well-positioned to support this requirement based on our [key differentiator] and track record of [relevant experience].

What to Include

  • Clear statement that you're interested and capable
  • Highlight your certification status upfront
  • Preview your key differentiator
SLIDE 9

Section 3: Capability Statement

CAPABILITY OVERVIEW:

[Company] has provided [service type] services since [year]. Our core capabilities include:

• [Capability 1]
• [Capability 2]
• [Capability 3]

RELEVANT EXPERIENCE:
We have successfully performed similar work for [Agency/Client].

Key Points

  • Match capabilities to their requirements
  • Quantify when possible ($, years, projects)
  • Include federal AND commercial experience
  • Keep it relevant — no fluff

Don't Oversell

This isn't a proposal. Be honest about your capabilities.

SLIDE 10

Section 4: Past Performance Summary

RELEVANT PAST PERFORMANCE:

Contract 1:
Client: [Agency/Company Name]
Value: [$X,XXX,XXX]
Period: [Start - End]
Services: [Brief description of work]
Relevance: [How this relates to the requirement]

Contract 2: [Same format...]

No Federal Past Performance?

Include commercial work, subcontracting experience, or state/local contracts. Label clearly as "Commercial" or "Subcontract."

SLIDE 11

Section 5: Questions & Feedback

QUESTIONS FOR CLARIFICATION:

1. [Question about scope]
2. [Question about timeline]
3. [Question about requirements]

RECOMMENDATIONS:
Based on our experience, we recommend [suggestion] to [benefit to government].

This is Your Influence

  • Ask smart questions — shows expertise
  • Suggest set-aside if applicable
  • Recommend bundling/unbundling
  • Identify unclear requirements

Power Move

"We recommend this be set aside for small businesses, as the scope aligns well with small business capabilities."

SLIDE 12

Section 6: Professional Closing

CLOSING:

We appreciate the opportunity to respond to this Sources Sought and would welcome the chance to discuss our capabilities further. Please feel free to contact me directly with any questions.

Respectfully submitted,

[Your Name]
[Title]
[Phone]
[Email]

Final Checklist

  • Include direct contact info (not generic inbox)
  • Attach capability statement (1-2 pages max)
  • Submit before deadline — early is better
SLIDE 13

Common Mistakes to Avoid

DON'T

  • Write a full proposal (too long)
  • Include pricing information
  • Claim capabilities you don't have
  • Submit after the deadline
  • Use generic template without customization

DO

  • Keep it 2-4 pages max
  • Customize for each opportunity
  • Highlight relevant certifications
  • Answer their specific questions
  • Follow their format instructions
