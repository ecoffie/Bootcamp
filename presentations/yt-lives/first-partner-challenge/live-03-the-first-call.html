Why Most Cold Calls Fail

What People Do Wrong

  • Start with a pitch, not a question
  • Talk about themselves first
  • Ask for too much too soon
  • Sound like they're reading a script
  • No clear "ask" at the end

What Actually Works

  • Lead with curiosity
  • Show you did research
  • Ask for just 2 minutes
  • Sound like a conversation
  • Clear next step

Step 1: The Opening (15 seconds)

Say This:

"Hi, this is [YOUR NAME] with [YOUR COMPANY]. I'm a small business in federal contracting, and I came across your company while researching [THEIR NAICS/INDUSTRY]. Do you have 2 minutes for a quick question?"

Why It Works

You're not selling. You're asking a question. People say yes to 2 minutes.

Key Phrase

"Quick question" is non-threatening. It opens the door without pressure.

Step 2: The Bridge (30 seconds)

If They Say Yes:

"Great, thanks. I saw that you've done work with [AGENCY or CONTRACT] and your capabilities in [AREA]. I'm looking to connect with companies who might be open to teaming on future opportunities. Are you currently exploring teaming relationships for 2026?"

Show Your Research

Mentioning specific work they've done proves you're not just dialing random numbers.

The Question

Asking about THEIR plans opens dialogue. It's not "let me tell you about me."

Step 3: The Value Exchange (60 seconds)

When They Ask "What Do You Do?":

"We're a [YOUR CERT] [SIZE] business specializing in [YOUR NAICS/SERVICES]. We've done [YOUR EXPERIENCE]. I'm specifically looking for partners who need our certification for set-asides, could use our technical capabilities, or are on vehicles we could subcontract through. Would any of those fit?"

The 3 Value Hooks

1. Certification value - Your certs help them win set-asides
2. Technical value - You have skills they need
3. Vehicle access - You can sub through their vehicles

Step 4: The Close (15 seconds)

If Interested:

"That's great. What's the best way to continue this? Can I send my capability statement and schedule a 15-minute call this week?"

If Not Right Now:

"I appreciate the honesty. Would you be open to connecting on LinkedIn or staying in touch for future opportunities?"

Always Get Something

Even a "no" can become a LinkedIn connection. That's still a win. Stay in their orbit.

Handling Common Objections

"We're not looking for partners right now"

"Completely understand. Things change quickly in GovCon. Mind if I send my capability statement for when opportunities do come up?"

"Send me an email"

"Will do. What email should I use, and is there anything specific you'd want me to address?"

"What's in it for us?"

"Fair question. I can bring [YOUR CERT] for set-aside compliance and [YOUR SKILL] for technical depth. I'm looking for mutually beneficial relationships."

"We only work with companies we know"

"That makes sense. This call is the start of getting to know each other. Would a 15-minute video call help?"

Getting Past the Gatekeeper

Option 1: Direct Ask

"Hi, I'm trying to reach someone in business development or your small business liaison. Who handles teaming partnerships?"

Option 2: Name Drop

"I was researching [COMPANY]'s work on [CONTRACT] and wanted to discuss a potential teaming opportunity. Who's the right person?"

Pro Tips

  • Be friendly, not demanding
  • Gatekeepers can help if you ask nicely
  • Get a name even if they're not available
  • Ask for email if phone is blocked
  • Call back at different times

Leaving a Voicemail That Gets Returned

The 20-Second Voicemail:

"Hi [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with [COMPANY]. I'm a [CERT] small business looking to connect about teaming opportunities in [NAICS/AREA]. I saw your work with [AGENCY] and think there might be fit. My number is [NUMBER]. Again, [YOUR NAME], [NUMBER]. I'll send a quick email too."

Key Elements

  • Say your number TWICE
  • Keep it under 30 seconds
  • Reference something specific
  • Mention the follow-up email

Follow-Up Rhythm

Call → Voicemail → Email same day
Wait 3 days → Call again
Wait 1 week → Final attempt

The Right Mindset

You're not asking for a favor.
You're offering a business opportunity.

Remember

Primes NEED small business partners. Your certifications and capabilities have VALUE. You're not begging—you're proposing a mutually beneficial partnership.

Practice Before the Challenge

  • Read the script out loud 10 times
  • Record yourself and listen back
  • Practice with a friend or family member
  • Time yourself (should be ~2 minutes total)
  • Customize with YOUR certifications and capabilities

On Challenge Day

You'll have live coaching and support. But the more you practice beforehand, the more confident you'll sound on real calls.

After Every Call

Immediately

  • Log the call in your tracker
  • Note their response & next steps
  • Set follow-up reminder
  • Send email if promised

Within 24 Hours

  • Send capability statement
  • Connect on LinkedIn
  • Calendar the follow-up
  • Research next opportunity to mention

The Fortune is in the Follow-Up

Most partnerships don't happen on the first call. Consistent follow-up is what converts contacts into contracts.
