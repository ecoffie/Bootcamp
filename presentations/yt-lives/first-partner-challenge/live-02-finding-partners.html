Tool #1: SAM.gov Entity Search

How to Use It

  • Go to SAM.gov → Search → Entity Information
  • Filter by your NAICS codes
  • Filter by state/location
  • Filter by business type (Large, Small, 8(a), etc.)
  • Export results to spreadsheet

Pro Tip

Look for LARGE businesses in your NAICS - they need small business subs to meet goals. Also look for small businesses with contract vehicles you want access to.

What to Capture

Company name, UEI, NAICS codes, POC name, phone, email, certifications

Tool #2: USASpending.gov

The Secret Weapon

  • Shows WHO is winning contracts NOW
  • See contract values and agencies
  • Filter by NAICS, agency, location
  • Find primes with active work = active subbing needs

Search Strategy

Look for companies that won contracts in the last 12 months in your NAICS. These are ACTIVE buyers of subcontractor services.

Bonus: Sub Plans

Large contracts ($750K+) require subcontracting plans. These primes MUST use small businesses.

Tool #3: SBA Dynamic Small Business Search

For Finding Certified SBs

  • dsbs.sba.gov
  • Search by certification type
  • Search by NAICS code
  • Search by state/keywords
  • Find complementary businesses

Why It Matters

Sometimes the best partners are other small businesses. Team together to bid on larger opportunities neither could win alone.

Look For

Companies with certifications you DON'T have. Your 8(a) + their HUBZone = more opportunities.

Tool #4: LinkedIn (Done Right)

Strategic Search

  • Search "small business liaison" + company
  • Search "BD manager" + federal contractor
  • Search "teaming" + your industry
  • Follow GovCon hashtags for active players

SBLM = Small Business Liaison Manager

Every large prime has one. Their JOB is to find small business partners. They WANT to hear from you.

Connection Strategy

Research first, then send personalized connection request. Mention specific opportunity or capability match.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Ask for money upfront to "partner"
  • Vague about actual opportunities
  • Want your info but share nothing
  • No recent contract activity
  • Won't sign teaming agreement
  • Push you to "just trust us"
