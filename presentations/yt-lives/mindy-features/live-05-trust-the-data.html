Why It Actually Costs You

A fabricated NAICS

You chase a market that isn't yours — weeks of capture work spent on a pursuit you can't win. A wasted pursuit.

A made-up dollar figure

You misjudge the prize, mis-price the bid, or cite a number in writing that the CO can disprove in one click — non-compliant.

Generic AI sounds confident. The penalty for being confidently wrong is the contract.
