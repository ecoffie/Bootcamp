Obligated vs. Ceiling — The Real Prize

Obligated

What's been spent so far — often just one task order on a much bigger vehicle. The small number.

Ceiling

The maximum the IDIQ can be worth across its life. This is the prize you're really fighting for.

A task order lives under a parent IDIQ vehicle. Judge the opportunity by the ceiling, not the one order you can see today.
