NAICS vs PSC

NAICS = WHO the seller is

The vendor's industry bucket.

Drones top NAICS: "Aircraft Manufacturing" — a catch-all that also holds jets and helicopters.

PSC = WHAT was bought

The literal product.

Drones top PSC: "1550 Unmanned Aircraft" — exactly what you sell. Pros search by what's bought.
