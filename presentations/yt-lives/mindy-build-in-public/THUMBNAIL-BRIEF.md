# Thumbnail + Title Production Brief — Mindy "Build in Public" Series

**Channel:** GovCon Giants | **Series:** Mindy — Build in Public | **Deliverable:** 5 thumbnails @ 1280x720

This brief covers **5 YouTube thumbnails** for the Mindy "Build in Public" livestream series on the GovCon Giants channel. Every thumbnail rides the **PURPLE brand system** (dark navy `#0f172a` base, indigo `#4338ca`/`#6366f1`, purple `#8b5cf6`/`#a78bfa`, green accent `#10b981`, white text). **Eric Coffie's face is the human anchor** in every frame — he is the real founder shipping a real product, and his presence is what separates this from a faceless AI/SaaS channel. Each thumbnail leads with a **big-number or bold-claim hook** (the emotional, scroll-stopping half of the pitch); the YouTube title carries the context and the reason to click. Mindy is the AI that scans **24,000+ federal contracts a night** — that scale is the recurring proof point.

---

## Global Rules

Pulled from the source "Thumbnail + Title Strategy" and "Before You Post" notes. These are non-negotiable across all 5.

- **Thumbnail and title are two halves of one pitch.** Thumbnail = emotional hook (big number / bold claim / scroll-stopper). Title = context, specificity, and the reason to click. Together they answer "What is this?" and "Why should I watch?"
- **Never repeat the same words in thumbnail vs. title.** The viewer sees them side-by-side. If they say the same thing, you've wasted half your real estate. (e.g., Stream 1 thumbnail says "REPLACE SAM.GOV" so the title does NOT — it adds "while the government was sleeping.")
- **First ~150 characters of the description matter most.** YouTube shows only that much before "Show more." That opening line is the hook — it is NOT a design task, but the designer should know the thumbnail/title/description work as one unit.
- **getmindy.ai link goes FIRST in every description.** It is the #1 conversion driver. (Context note for the production team, not the designer.)
- **Category:** Science & Technology.
- **Visibility / scheduling:** Public. Schedule the stream **15–30 minutes before start time** so subscribers get the notification.
- **Safe zone — keep text OUT of the bottom-right corner.** YouTube stamps the video duration there; any text or the big number placed there gets covered. Keep critical text in the left two-thirds and upper area.
- **Legibility:** Large, bold, readable font. Aim for text that survives the mobile feed (small render). Big number / claim should be the single largest element.
- **Contrast on purple:** White text with a subtle dark stroke or drop shadow over the purple/indigo gradient; reserve **green `#10b981`** for the one word/number you want the eye to hit first. Do not let purple text sit on purple background.
- **Consistency:** Same face-crop style, same logo lockup placement, and same font family across all 5 so the series reads as a set in the feed.

## Per-Stream Breakdown

### Stream 1 — "Replace SAM.gov"

- **Thumbnail Text:** `AN AI TO REPLACE SAM.GOV`
- **YouTube Title:** `I Built This While the Government Was Sleeping (Live Coding)`
- **Hook strategy:** Controversy + mystery. The thumbnail screams "SAM.gov is getting replaced" (controversy / pattern-interrupt). The title adds the curiosity gap — "while the government was sleeping."
- **Design direction:**
  - **Layout:** Eric's face cutout on the RIGHT third, looking toward the text. Big bold claim stacked on the LEFT/center.
  - **Big element:** "REPLACE SAM.GOV" is the dominant treatment — "REPLACE" in white, "SAM.GOV" in green `#10b981` so the eye lands on the target being killed.
  - **Color emphasis:** Dark navy `#0f172a` → indigo `#4338ca` gradient background; a faint, dimmed/glitched SAM.gov-blue UI screenshot behind the text reinforces "the old way" being replaced.
  - **Prop / expression:** Eric confident / slightly defiant — a knowing half-smile. Optional: a red "X" or strike motif over the dimmed SAM.gov UI.

### Stream 2 — "$750B"

- **Thumbnail Text:** `$750B`
- **YouTube Title:** `Coding an AI for the Biggest Market Nobody Talks About (Day 1)`
- **Hook strategy:** Number shock + insider knowledge. The thumbnail is just a massive `$750B` (raw number shock). The title explains what it is — a market nobody talks about (insider knowledge).
- **Design direction:**
  - **Layout:** `$750B` is the HERO — it fills the majority of the frame, center or center-left. Eric's face smaller, lower-left or lower-right (out of the bottom-right duration zone), reacting to the number.
  - **Big element:** `$750B` in massive bold white with a green `#10b981` glow or the `$` / `B` accented in green. This is the most number-forward of the five — let it breathe.
  - **Color emphasis:** Deep purple `#8b5cf6` → navy radial gradient so the white number pops hard. Subtle "Day 1" tag in a small indigo pill, top-left.
  - **Prop / expression:** Eric pointing up at the number, or hands-out "can you believe this" gesture. Eyebrows up — astonishment sells the scale.

### Stream 3 — "24,000 Contracts a Night"

- **Thumbnail Text:** `24,000 CONTRACTS A NIGHT`
- **YouTube Title:** `I Built an AI That Never Sleeps — Here's How She Works (Live)`
- **Hook strategy:** Scale + character intrigue. The thumbnail shows the feat — 24,000 contracts a night (scale). The title personifies the AI — "an AI that never sleeps" (character intrigue, "She").
- **Design direction:**
  - **Layout:** "24,000" enormous across the top/center, "CONTRACTS A NIGHT" smaller beneath it. Eric on the right third.
  - **Big element:** "24,000" is the dominant number — white with green `#10b981` accent. Treat it as a glowing counter / readout to imply machine speed.
  - **Color emphasis:** Night-time navy `#0f172a` dominant (it's "a night"), with indigo `#6366f1` and purple `#a78bfa` light streaks suggesting data flowing in the dark. A faint moon / "while you sleep" cue is on-brand.
  - **Prop / expression:** Eric calm/assured (the AI does the work while he rests). Optional small Mindy avatar/orb glowing purple to personify "She."

### Stream 4 — "Day 4 of 5 / Building Live" (EVERGREEN — reframed)

- **Thumbnail Text:** `DAY 4 OF 5` (primary) — alternative: `BUILDING LIVE`
- **YouTube Title:** `Can I Ship a Full SaaS Product in 9 Days? (Building Mindy Live)`
- **Hook strategy:** Urgency + stakes. Originally the source used a launch countdown ("9 DAYS TO LAUNCH"). **We are EVERGREEN now — do NOT use a countdown-to-launch.** Reframe the urgency as "in the trenches, building live" rather than a moving launch date. The thumbnail conveys momentum/stakes ("DAY 4 OF 5" or "BUILDING LIVE"); the title keeps the "can I actually ship it?" tension. Avoid any specific calendar date or "X days to launch."
- **Design direction:**
  - **Layout:** Eric center-left at his desk/keyboard (the "in the build" shot). Bold text top-right area, keeping clear of the bottom-right duration stamp.
  - **Big element:** "DAY 4 OF 5" stacked — "DAY 4" huge in white, "OF 5" smaller in green `#10b981`. If using "BUILDING LIVE," set "LIVE" in green with a small red live-dot.
  - **Color emphasis:** Indigo `#4338ca` → purple `#8b5cf6` gradient; code/editor glow on Eric's face to sell "building live." Keep it grittier than the others — this is the "real work, no highlight reel" episode.
  - **Prop / expression:** Eric heads-down or mid-keystroke, focused/intense (not posed). Optional: a faint code editor behind him. **No countdown clock, no launch date.**

### Stream 5 — "Their Replacement" (David vs. Goliath)

- **Thumbnail Text:** `LOCKHEED HAS 47 PEOPLE / THEIR REPLACEMENT`
- **YouTube Title:** `One AI vs. a 47-Person Team — Building the $149/mo Alternative (Live)`
- **Hook strategy:** David vs. Goliath + value. The thumbnail names the enemy — Lockheed, 47 people (the Goliath). The title makes the value tangible — `$149/mo` vs. an army (value prop). Thumbnail and title split the math so nothing repeats.
- **Design direction:**
  - **Layout:** Split / VS composition. LEFT: "LOCKHEED — 47 PEOPLE" (the giant). RIGHT: Eric's face + "THEIR REPLACEMENT." A center "VS" or divider sells the matchup.
  - **Big element:** "47" is the shock number on the left (white, large); "THEIR REPLACEMENT" is the punch line on the right in green `#10b981`.
  - **Color emphasis:** Left side cooler/darker navy `#0f172a` (corporate Goliath); right side warmer purple `#8b5cf6`/`#a78bfa` (Mindy/Eric, the challenger). Green only on "REPLACEMENT" so the underdog claim wins the eye.
  - **Prop / expression:** Eric confident, arms crossed or pointing at himself. Optional faint Lockheed-style corporate-building silhouette on the left vs. a single glowing Mindy orb on the right (one AI vs. an army).
