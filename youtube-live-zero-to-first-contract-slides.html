STEP 1

Get Registered

SAM.gov
System for Award Management — required for all federal contracts
UEI
Unique Entity Identifier — your federal business ID number
CAGE
Commercial & Government Entity code — assigned automatically

This is your license to sell to the government.
Free. Takes 30-60 minutes. No registration = no contracts.

STEP 3

Research Target Agencies

Don't bid on everything. TARGET 3-5 agencies that buy what you sell.

1 Use Market Assassin: 5 inputs, 8 reports in 30 seconds
2 Agency spending — how much they spend in your NAICS
3 Pain points — what problems they need solved
4 Competition — who else is bidding
5 Budget trends — is spending going up or down

30 seconds of research replaces 4-6 weeks of guessing.

STEP 5

Build Relationships

73% of contracts go to KNOWN vendors.

Agency SBLOs
Small Business Liaison Officers — your door into every agency. Use the SBLO Contact Directory.
Prime SBLOs
Large contractor small business offices — your path to subcontracts. Use the Federal Contractor Database.
Industry Days
Conferences, matchmaking events, and capability briefings. Show up in person.

Stop chasing contracts. Start building relationships.

STEP 6

Respond to Opportunities

Three types — ranked by difficulty

1. Sources Sought / RFIs
EASIEST — Just capability info
Less than 5% of contractors respond
Start here.
2. Simplified Acquisitions
MODERATE — Under $250K
Shorter proposals
Less competition
3. Full & Open / Task Orders
ADVANCED — Full proposals
Most competitive
Graduate to this

Start with #1. Graduate to #3.

All three are valid. All three build past performance.
