Here's something that surprises most contractors:

50% of federal contracting opportunities never hit SAM.gov.

They're called task orders — and they're issued under IDIQ vehicles (Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity contracts).

Here's how it works:

1. An agency awards a "master contract" (the IDIQ) to multiple vendors

2. When they need specific work, they issue "task orders" ONLY to those vendors

3. These task orders are often NOT posted on SAM.gov

4. Instead, they're posted on eBuy, agency portals, or sent directly

If you're only searching SAM.gov, you're missing half the opportunities.

$77 BILLION IN EXPIRING IDIQs

Of the $221B expiring, $77B is in IDIQ task order vehicles.
These are some of the easiest wins if you know where to look.

The major IDIQ vehicles you should know:

> GSA MAS (Multiple Award Schedule) — the biggest vehicle

> OASIS+ — professional services across all agencies

> CIO-SP4 — IT services for civilian agencies

> Alliant 2 — IT solutions across government

> SEWP V — IT hardware and solutions
